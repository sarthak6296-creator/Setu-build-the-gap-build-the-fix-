# Demo video script (about 2.5 minutes)

**0:00 Intro (15 s)** "Hi, I am Sarthak from team Ai coder. This is OpportunityKit for PS-03. Students struggle to know if they qualify for internships, scholarships and competitions."

**0:15 Organize (30 s)** Open the live link. Paste the sample internship text and press Build checklist. Point at the highlighted phrases and the filled checklist. Change one field to show it is editable.

**0:45 Profile (25 s)** Tap 2nd year and CSE, move the CGPA slider to 8.2, add skills HTML, CSS, Python as tags, tap the resume card to Ready. Leave the GitHub link and weeks empty. Point at the live meter at the bottom. Say: "Blank means unknown, not fail."

**1:10 Result (35 s)** Press Check my fit. Show 3 matches, 1 mismatch, 2 missing info, the readiness bar and next steps. Read one reason: "JavaScript is not listed, learn it before applying."

**1:40 What-if (15 s)** Tick "What if I fix this?" on JavaScript, then on the two missing items. Watch the ring go from 50% to 100% and the verdict change to Ready. Say: "It shows exactly what to fix first."

**1:45 Other types (20 s)** Click Scholarship, then Competition. Show that the same checklist format works.

**2:05 Errors + phone (10 s)** Press Check with empty input to show the error message. Show the page on a phone.

**2:15 Business (20 s)** Scroll to Who pays and why: free plan, Student Plus, college license, and low running cost.

**2:35 Close (10 s)** "Thank you. Links to the live demo and GitHub are in the PDF."
