OpportunityKit

Paste what an internship, scholarship or competition asks for. Add your profile. See what matches, what doesn’t and what is still unknown.

1. Organize

Paste any internship, scholarship or competition text. Visible rules find year, CGPA, skills, documents, links, dates and more, and highlight them in your text.

2. Compare

Each requirement is checked against your profile with a plain rule, such as CGPA at or above the cut-off. A blank answer is Missing info, never a fail.

3. Explain

Every item gets a badge, a reason and a fix. You see a readiness bar and ordered next steps, not just yes or no.

Value versus cost

Students save hours of reading rules and avoid wrong or late applications. Colleges get better prepared applicants and higher placement readiness. Running cost is very low because the rules run in the browser and need no AI calls. Pilot idea: a small yearly fee per student for colleges (for example ₹99) and a small monthly fee for Student Plus (for example ₹49). These are assumptions to test with real users.
